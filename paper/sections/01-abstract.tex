\begin{abstract}
% [ERIC-REVIEW] Post-RT H3 mitigation : flag explicitly that one of three
% hypotheses is not externally published, to preempt "euphemism"
% critique. Please validate phrasing.
We present a Lean~4 formalisation of a conditional non-existence result
for nontrivial odd cycles in the Collatz dynamics. The result is stated as
\((\mathsf{B1} \wedge \mathsf{B2} \wedge \mathsf{B3}) \Rightarrow \neg\,
\text{(nontrivial odd cycle exists)}\), where
\(\mathsf{B1}\) is a structural witness for Baker's theorem on linear
forms in logarithms~\cite{Baker1966} with constant \(\mu = 6\) for
\(\log_2 3\) (a weakening of the finer Rhin bound~\cite{Rhin1987}),
\(\mathsf{B2}\) is a structural witness for Barina's computational
verification of the Collatz conjecture up to \(2^{71}\)~\cite{Barina2025},
and \(\mathsf{B3}\) is a \emph{derived} --- \emph{not externally
published} --- bound of the form \(\mathtt{IsOddCycle}\,n\,k \wedge k >
1322 \Rightarrow n < 2^{71}\), motivated by the
continued-fraction expansion of \(\log_2 3\) and the subject of planned
future work (see Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}). The Lean~4 formalisation
(toolchain \texttt{v4.27.0}, Mathlib \texttt{v4.27.0}) contains zero
\texttt{sorry} in the central theorem chain, and its axiom list is
\([\text{\texttt{propext}},\, \text{\texttt{Classical.choice}},\,
\text{\texttt{Quot.sound}}]\) --- the three standard kernel axioms used
by essentially every non-constructive Mathlib theorem. The repository
ships with an end-to-end reproducibility script and a continuous
integration pipeline that enforce this baseline on every commit. We
describe the explicit separation between published and derived hypotheses,
the Lean-level \texttt{structure}-versus-\texttt{axiom} design choice, and
the work required to promote \(\mathsf{B3}\) to a proven theorem.
\end{abstract}
