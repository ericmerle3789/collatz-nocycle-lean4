\begin{abstract}
% [ERIC-REVIEW] phrasing, positioning claims vs field. Please confirm or amend.
We present a Lean~4 formalisation of the non-existence of nontrivial odd cycles
in the Collatz dynamics, conditional on three explicit external hypotheses :
Baker's theorem on linear forms in logarithms~\cite{Baker1966} (with constant
\(\mu = 6\) for \(\log_2 3\), a weakening of the finer Rhin bound~\cite{Rhin1987}),
Barina's computational verification of the Collatz conjecture up to
\(2^{71}\)~\cite{Barina2025}, and a continued-fractions-derived bound on the
seed of a potential large-\(k\) cycle, which we state as a named structure.
The central theorem is formalised in
Lean~4 (toolchain \texttt{v4.27.0}) with Mathlib~\texttt{v4.27.0},
contains \emph{zero} \texttt{sorry}, and its axiom chain is
\([\text{\texttt{propext}},\, \text{\texttt{Classical.choice}},\,
\text{\texttt{Quot.sound}}]\) --- the three standard kernel axioms of
Mathlib, and no others. The repository ships with an end-to-end
reproducibility script and a continuous integration pipeline that enforce
this baseline on every commit. We state explicitly which hypotheses are
published, which are derived, and what work would be required to replace
the derived hypothesis with a fully formalised theorem.
\end{abstract}
